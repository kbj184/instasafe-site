Frequently asked questions

Will I get banned for using InstaSafe?

InstaSafe is built specifically to reduce that risk. It never uses your password, works at a randomized human-like pace (30–60 seconds between actions), and enforces a daily limit that stops automatically. You unfollow gradually — the way a careful person would.

Do you see my Instagram password?

No. InstaSafe runs inside your own browser where you're already logged in. It never asks for, sees, or stores your password or login details.

Where is my follower data processed?

Entirely on your own computer, inside your browser. Your follower/following lists and backup files are never uploaded to any server.

How do I get my Instagram data?

Instagram → Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information → select “Followers and following.” InstaSafe reads this export locally. You can also use the live-scan mode with no download.

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free lets you find every non-follower and safely clean up your top 15. Pro ($4.99/month) removes the limit so you can clean up your whole list, on up to 5 devices, and cancel anytime.
